# Standard Operating Procedure — Customer Due Diligence (v4.2, excerpt)

Section 4 — Identification and verification

4.1 Every customer must be identified and verified before any account is opened or any transaction is processed.

4.2 A passport, national identity card or equivalent official photographic document shall be obtained and its validity recorded at the time of onboarding.

4.3 Where the customer's address is verified, the supporting document must be dated within 90 days of the application date.

Section 5 — Beneficial ownership

5.1 The firm must identify every natural person who ultimately owns or controls 25 per cent or more of the customer's shares or voting rights.

5.2 Where no natural person meets the ownership threshold, the firm shall identify the natural person exercising control by other means and record the basis for that determination.

5.3 Where a shareholder is a nominee, the firm must obtain a declaration identifying the underlying principal before the relationship is established.

5.4 Ownership chains must be traced to a natural person; where the chain cannot be completed the case must be referred to the enhanced due diligence unit.

Section 6 — Screening

6.1 All beneficial owners, directors and authorised signatories must be screened against sanctions lists, politically exposed person lists and adverse media at onboarding and at every periodic review.

6.2 A potential sanctions match may not be cleared by an analyst below the level of the MLRO.

6.3 Where a screening list is unavailable, the case must not be treated as cleared and the MLRO must be notified within one business day.

Section 7 — Risk assessment

7.1 Every case must be assigned a risk rating using the approved methodology and the rating must be recorded with the factors that produced it.

7.2 The firm must not apply a lower risk rating on the basis of a single mitigating factor where an elevated-risk factor is present.

7.3 Enhanced due diligence must be completed within 30 days of the relationship being established where the customer presents a high risk.

Section 8 — Documentation

8.1 The rationale for every decision must be documented and retained for five years after the relationship ends.

8.2 Any deviation from this procedure requires documented approval by a senior compliance officer.
